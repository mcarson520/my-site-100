About me

✨ Welcome to Brickell Ballroom! ✨

I'm Megan, your Brickell Ballroom Dance instructor! I grew up dancing ballet, jazz, and lyrical, and watching Dancing with the Stars, but I always assumed ballroom was only for the rich or retired. Then in 2015 I discovered the ballroom club at Oakland University and fell in love with the variety of styles and the challenge of navigating the floor with a partner. I went on to perform, compete, and teach the beginner class.

After moving to Miami and trying bachata and zouk, I knew ballroom was what I really wanted to dance. So I started Brickell Ballroom Dance to create an accessible space for the Miami community to learn and enjoy it. No partner or experience needed. Everyone is welcome! 💃🕺🏼✨
